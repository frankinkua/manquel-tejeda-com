# Research Lessons

- **Lesson:** Over-reliance on generic search engines or standard APIs leads to rate limits and anti-bot CAPTCHAs. Relying on DuckDuckGo Lite HTML and cached search snippets is an effective alternative when primary platforms (like Twitter/X) block automated scrapers.
- **Improved prompt clause:** "When searching for specific individuals or media on heavily bot-protected platforms, prioritize DuckDuckGo Lite HTML to retrieve cached search snippets as initial leads, and strictly document the inability to access primary sources rather than failing the task."
