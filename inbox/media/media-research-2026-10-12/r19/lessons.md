# Research Lessons

- Even when a specific PDF program is found, accompanying presentation decks or video pitches may not be publicly indexed, especially for virtual investor networking events.
- Tools like Wayback Machine might not comprehensively capture dynamically generated event pages or unlisted media for specific pitch events.

**Improved Prompt Clause:**
"When searching for virtual pitch event media, verify if the event organizer publishes pitch recordings publicly (e.g., on a known YouTube channel) before assuming video evidence exists alongside the program PDF."
