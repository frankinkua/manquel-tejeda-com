02 / The person behind the ideas

mt↗

Frankfurt am Main, Germany

Germany↔Chile↔Taiwan

A Frankfurt spirit.
An international outlook.

Born in Chile. Raised in Frankfurt. An entrepreneurial journey shaped by different places, people, and possibilities.

Manquel’s first startup entered the Goethe University incubator in 2000. His path has since taken him through ventures in Germany, Chile, and Taiwan, bringing a business background to technology-led ideas.

He studied at Goethe University Frankfurt, Universidad Complutense de Madrid, and Universidad de Concepción.
